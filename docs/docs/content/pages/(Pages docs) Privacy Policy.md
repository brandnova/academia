# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 2026*

Academia Atlas is built and maintained by Brand Nova. This policy explains what information we
collect on the Academia Atlas website and in the Academia Atlas mobile app (together, "the platform"),
why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

**When you sign in with Google**, we receive your email address, full name, and profile photo
(if you have one set on your Google account). That's the entire sign-in process. We never see
or store a password, because we don't handle passwords at all. On the mobile app, Google's
sign-in opens in Google's own screen or your device's browser, and we receive the same details
and nothing more.

**When you use the platform**, we store what you actually do with it: questions, answers,
comments, votes, follows, and reports you submit. This content is necessary for the platform
to function as a knowledge base, that's the whole product.

**Technical information on the website**: your session is maintained through a secure,
browser-managed cookie that identifies you as logged in. It isn't readable by JavaScript on any
page, including ours, and it isn't used for advertising or tracking.

**Technical information in the mobile app**: after you sign in, the app keeps your session
tokens in your device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore). They stay on
your device, are used only to keep you signed in, and are removed when you sign out. The app
also stores a few things on your device so it works smoothly: a copy of your basic profile
(name, email and photo link) and your light or dark mode choice.

**Request information**: like any web service, our servers and hosting providers handle
standard request details such as your IP address, the time and the page or action requested.
We use this to keep the platform secure and to apply rate limits that prevent abuse.

## What we don't collect

We don't collect payment information, we don't currently ask for anything beyond what Google
provides at sign-in, and we don't use third-party advertising trackers or sell any data to
anyone. The mobile app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, photos or
microphone, and it contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

## How we use it

To run your account and show your name/photo on content you post, to notify you about activity
relevant to you (someone answered your question, for example) through in-app notifications and
email, to keep the platform safe (reviewing reports, moderating content), and to understand how
the platform is actually used so we can improve it. The mobile app does not use push
notifications at the moment. If that changes, we will ask your permission first and update this
policy.

## Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. It's shared only with the services that make the
platform work:

- **Google**, for the sign-in process itself.
- **Our hosting, database and email providers**, who store, serve and deliver the platform's
  data and messages on our behalf. Some of this infrastructure may be located outside Nigeria,
  standard for most web platforms, your data is still governed by this policy regardless of
  where it's physically stored.
- **Apple and Google**, when you download the app through their stores, under their own
  privacy policies. We don't receive details about you from them beyond aggregate download
  statistics.

We may also disclose information if genuinely required by law, or to protect the safety of our
users.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and activity data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like
your account and its content removed, contact us at brandnova.dev@gmail.com from the email
address on your account and we'll process that request. Note that because questions and answers
are connected to a shared knowledge base other students may rely on, we'll let you know what
specifically would be removed versus what might need to stay (for example, an answer that other
students are actively relying on) before completing a deletion request. Signing out of the
mobile app removes your session from the device, and uninstalling the app removes everything the
app stored on it.

## Your rights

You can review and correct your name at any time from your profile page, on the website or in
the app. You can request a copy of your data, or request deletion, by contacting us at
brandnova.dev@gmail.com. We aim to respond to any request like this within a reasonable time.

## Security

Your session is secured using industry-standard practices: encrypted connections (HTTPS),
secure session cookies that can't be read by scripts on the website, secure device storage for
session tokens in the mobile app, and rate limiting to prevent abuse. Because we never store a
password, a whole category of credential-related risk simply doesn't apply to Academia Atlas accounts.

## Children's privacy

Academia Atlas is built for tertiary (post-secondary) students. We don't knowingly collect
information from anyone below the minimum age required to access tertiary education in their
country.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top and, where
appropriate, let users know through the platform.
