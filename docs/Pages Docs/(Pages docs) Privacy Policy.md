Academia is built and maintained by Brand Nova. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

**When you sign in with Google**, we receive your email address, full name, and profile photo (if you have one set on your Google account). That's the entire sign-in process, we never see or store a password, Academia doesn't have one to leak, because we don't handle passwords at all.

**When you use the platform**, we store what you actually do with it: questions, answers, comments, votes, follows, and reports you submit. This content is necessary for the platform to function as a knowledge base, that's the whole product.

**Technical information**: your session is maintained through a secure, browser-managed cookie that identifies you as logged in. It isn't readable by JavaScript on any page, including ours, and it isn't used for advertising or tracking, it exists purely to keep you signed in.

## What we don't collect

We don't collect payment information, we don't currently ask for anything beyond what Google provides at sign-in, and we don't use third-party advertising trackers or sell any data to anyone.

## How we use it

To run your account and show your name/photo on content you post, to notify you about activity relevant to you (someone answered your question, for example), to keep the platform safe (reviewing reports,  moderating content), and to understand how the platform is actually used so we can improve it.

## Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. It's shared only with the services that make the platform work:

- **Google**, for the sign-in process itself.
- **Our hosting and database providers**, who store and serve the platform's data on our behalf. Some of this infrastructure may be located outside Nigeria, standard for most web platforms, your data is still governed by this policy regardless of where it's physically stored.

We may also disclose information if genuinely required by law, or to protect the safety of our users.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and activity data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and its content removed, contact us at brandnova.dev@gmail.com and we'll process that request. Note that because questions and answers are connected to a shared knowledge base other students may rely on, we'll let you know what specifically would be removed versus what might need to stay (for example, an answer that other students are actively relying on) before completing a deletion request.

## Your rights

You can review and correct your name at any time from your profile page. You can request a copy of your data, or request deletion, by contacting us at brandnova.dev@gmail.com. We aim to respond to any request like this within a reasonable time.

## Security

Your session is secured using industry-standard practices: encrypted connections (HTTPS), secure session cookies that can't be read by scripts, and rate limiting to prevent abuse. Because we never store a password, a whole category of credential-related risk simply doesn't apply to Academia accounts.

## Children's privacy

Academia is built for tertiary (post-secondary) students. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone below the minimum age required to access tertiary education in their country.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top and, where appropriate, let users know through the platform.
