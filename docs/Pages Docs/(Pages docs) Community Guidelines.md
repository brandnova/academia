Academia exists for one reason: to help students find real answers to real academic questions, and to make sure those answers are still useful to the next student who needs them. These guidelines exist to protect that.

## The point of this platform

Ask genuine academic questions specific to your school, department, or situation. Answer honestly, with context, especially if you're not completely sure, say so, rather than guessing with confidence. A good answer here should still be useful to someone reading it two years from now.

## Before you ask

Search first. If a question has already been asked and answered, add to that thread (a comment, a vote) instead of creating a duplicate. Duplicate questions split useful answers across multiple threads instead of building one strong one.

Use tags that already exist where they fit. Creating a near-duplicate tag ("siwes" and "siwes-placement" as separate tags, for example) makes both harder to find later.

## When you answer

Answer what was actually asked. If you're not sure, say what you're not sure about, half-correct information stated confidently is worse than no answer at all on a platform meant to be trusted.

If someone else's answer is genuinely more complete or correct than yours, upvote it. Voting exists to surface the best answer, not to support whoever you know.

## What's not okay here

- **Spam or promotional content.** This isn't a place to advertise services, products, or unrelated links.
- **Abuse or harassment.** Disagree with an answer if you think it's wrong, don't attack the person who gave it.
- **Deliberate misinformation.** Answers about registration, GPA calculation, clearance, and similar processes affect real academic decisions. Don't guess and present it as fact.
- **Academic dishonesty.** Explaining a concept so someone understands it is exactly what this platform is for. Writing someone's assignment, giving live exam answers, or otherwise doing the academic work *for* someone isn't, and won't be tolerated.
- **Impersonation**, or misrepresenting your school, department, or role.

## Reporting content

If you see something that shouldn't be here, report it. You can flag a question, answer, or comment as spam, abuse, misinformation, or a duplicate. Reports go to admins for review, they're never shown publicly, and your identity as the reporter is never visible to the person you reported.

## If guidelines aren't followed

Content that breaks these guidelines can be removed. Accounts that repeatedly or seriously violate them can be suspended. This isn't about punishment, it's about keeping the platform trustworthy for the students who depend on it.

## One more thing

Academia isn't an official university system, and answers here come from other students, not your school's registrar. For anything that genuinely matters (deadlines, formal requirements, official processes), always confirm with your school's official channels. This platform is here to point you in the right direction and save you time, not to replace your school's own communication.
